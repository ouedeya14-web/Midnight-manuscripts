title: "Chapter 5 :"
---
After the trauma, Angela’s world shrank into silence. The girl who once laughed freely, who had wandered through her villa with a lightness in her step, became a quiet shadow. She no longer spoke about what she felt or experienced, locking her pain deep inside where no one could reach it. The words that had once flowed so easily now stuck in her throat, swallowed by fear and shame. 

Every glance from a boy made her chest tighten. Every whisper in the hallway sent a shiver crawling down her spine. Panic would grip her unexpectedly, her breath catching, her heart hammering so fiercely she feared it would escape her chest. Sometimes her hands trembled, and tears welled up behind her closed eyelids, but she never let anyone see. She learned to avoid hallways, classrooms, and the places where boys lingered—safe zones became tiny corners of emptiness where she could hide. 

Inside, the pain twisted into a cold, silent storm. It was a storm she bore alone, shielding herself from a world that had hurt her so deeply. She stopped asking for help. She stopped hoping. Silence became both her armor and her prison. 

Her eyes, once bright and curious, now held a distant, weary gaze. She moved through the villa like a ghost, always quiet, always careful not to attract attention. Laughter from her peers felt like a language she no longer understood. Touch, even a casual one, startled her. 

Yet within that quiet shell, Angela was learning survival. She had become fragile but resilient—a girl holding a shattered heart together with threads of quiet strength and secret sorrow. Each day, she practiced smiles she didn’t feel, conversations she didn’t want, gestures that hid the trembling storm within. 

The trauma had changed her, but no one truly saw how. Her parents noticed the silence, the withdrawal, but their concern was fleeting, quickly replaced by the bustle of their high-society lives. Only Angela knew the depth of her loneliness, the secret grief that followed her to her room at night, where she wept silently beneath silk sheets, a small girl carrying a shadow no one could touch. 

And so, Angela became a ghost in her own life, a girl living behind walls of silence, learning that pain could be endured—but never spoken.
