---
title: "Chapter 3 : The scar beneath the skin"
---
The day he asked Angela to meet him, her small heart fluttered with a mix of nervous excitement and unease. She had never seen him—only read his messages, soft, secretive, coaxing her to keep their friendship hidden. Each word had seemed gentle, almost kind, but a faint whisper of doubt tickled the back of her mind.

She waited alone in the shadowed garden behind her family's grand villa. The air was cool against her skin, carrying the scent of damp earth and blooming flowers, a sharp contrast to the unease gnawing at her chest. She tried to focus on the sunlight filtering through the trees, but her thoughts kept circling back to the unknown figure she was about to meet.

Then he appeared.

He was older—far older than her, a man in his late teens or early twenties, his presence commanding yet quiet. Angela's gaze was immediately drawn to the pale scar just beneath his neck, a thin line that seemed out of place, like a warning etched into his skin.

Her stomach twisted, a flicker of fear crawling up her spine. She didn't understand why it unsettled her so, only that it did.

His voice was low, coaxing, smooth like velvet. "Don't be afraid. It's just between us," he murmured, his words wrapping around her like a trap disguised as comfort.

Angela hesitated, a small part of her longing for friendship and attention clinging to the sweetness of his tone. But the instinctive warning in her chest tightened.

She felt powerless, fragile, and yet unable to step back as he drew closer.

The warmth of his hand turned to something cold and unyielding. His grip closed around her, forcing her into an experience her young mind could barely comprehend. Panic, confusion, and terror collided inside her. She froze, tears blurring her vision, her small body trembling against the weight of what was happening.

When it ended, he left as quietly as he had appeared, leaving only the memory of the scar beneath his neck burned into Angela's mind. It became a silent mark, a fragment of horror she could clutch onto when words failed her. A secret she dared never to speak aloud, even to her sister or parents.

That day, a part of Angela's childhood shattered. Her innocence, once bright and untouchable, had fractured, leaving a small, trembling girl carrying a shadow inside her.

The scar she remembered was more than skin deep—it was a silent signal of the darkness that had crept into her life, and the world she once knew would never feel safe in the same way again.
